# Marketplace listing

## Name

Sunset Arcade

## Description

Sunset Arcade turns the Stream Deck+ touch strip and dials into a tiny arcade. Play Breakout, Snake, 2-player Foosball and Sunset Racer, a pseudo-3D arcade racer with hills, curves and traffic, right on your desk. Every frame is drawn live on the touch strip and every game is played with the dials, so there is no window to keep open.

What is included
- Breakout: rotate a dial to move the paddle, press to launch the ball.
- Snake: rotate to turn, press to start or pause. Your best score is kept.
- Foosball: a 2-player table across the whole strip. Red plays dials 1 and 3, blue plays dials 2 and 4. Rotate to slide the rods, press to kick, hold to lift. First to 5 wins.
- Sunset Racer: rotate to steer, press for a speed boost (3 per race) and reach each checkpoint in time. Five stages, from Palm Beach to Autumn Road.
- Sunset Racer 4P: split-screen racing for up to four players, one per dial. Press to join.
- Racer Map: a live top-down map of the race with every player's position and ranking. Put it on several keys for one big map. Press to zoom on the leader.

Setup tips
- Drag a game onto a dial. Foosball and Racer 4P need all four dials of one page.
- Touch the strip to pause or resume, long-touch to restart or return to the title.

Requirements
- Stream Deck+ (the games need its dials and touch strip)
- Stream Deck 7.1 or later, on Windows 10 or macOS 12 or later

Code and text written with AI assistance (Claude). All graphics are drawn by the plugin; no AI-generated images.
