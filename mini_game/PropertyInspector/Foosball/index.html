How to play
Controls

Put Foosball on all 4 dials.
RED: dial 1 + 3, BLUE: dial 2 + 4
Rotate: move rods up / down
Press dial: kick
Hold dial: lift players (ball passes under)
Tap strip: pause / resume
Long press strip: back to title
Goals to Win is shared, the last change wins.
